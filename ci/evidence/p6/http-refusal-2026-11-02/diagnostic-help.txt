usage: http_probe.py [-h] [--output OUTPUT]

Three bounded GETs, no acquisition/install/build or environment-value dump.
Compare default curl, installed libostree identification, and the driver's
clean environment. Raw response headers/bodies are private; public output is
allowlisted. No changing User-Agent is proposed as an acquisition workaround.

options:
  -h, --help       show this help message and exit
  --output OUTPUT
